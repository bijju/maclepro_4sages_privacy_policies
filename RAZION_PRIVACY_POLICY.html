Razion Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026
Publisher: MACLEPRO INC ("we", "us")
Applies to: the Razion mobile game for Android (package com.sagez.razion) and for iOS and iPadOS (bundle com.sagez.razion)

This policy explains what information Razion handles, why, and what choices you have. Razion is designed to keep your data on your device wherever possible. Where a Google or Apple service is involved, we say so plainly below. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Summary

  • Razion does not require an account and has no servers of its own.
  • Your game progress, settings, upgrades, unlocked items and local high scores are stored on your device.
  • On Android, if you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, a copy of your pilot profile is saved to your own Google account so it can follow you between devices. The iOS version has no cloud save and no sign-in.
  • The game shows advertising through Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier and related data, and you can control whether ads are personalised.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing on Android and by Apple's App Store on iOS. We never see your payment details.
  • Razion contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no third-party ad mediation networks and no social features.

2. Information stored on your device

Razion keeps a local save file containing your progress, settings, upgrades, purchased and unlocked items, and a leaderboard of your own best runs. This file never leaves your device unless you turn on cloud save (section 3, Android only). Uninstalling the game deletes it.

3. Google Play Games sign-in and cloud save (Android only)

Sign-in is optional. If you sign in with Google Play Games Services, Razion receives your Play Games player ID and display name so it can greet you and match a saved profile to you. It does not receive your email address, contacts or Google account password.

While signed in, the game stores one copy of your pilot profile (the same data as the local save file) in the app-data area of your Google account, using Google's Saved Games feature. Only Razion can read that storage, and it is held by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not operate a server and cannot access your cloud profile.

You can stop cloud saving by signing out of Play Games in the game. To delete the cloud copy, remove Razion's data from your Google account at Google Account → Data & privacy → Third-party apps & services, or delete your Play Games profile in the Play Games app.

The iOS version does not offer sign-in or cloud save, and does not use Game Center or iCloud.

4. Advertising

Razion shows interstitial and rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in those ads, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID, or on iOS the IDFA where you have allowed tracking) and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate (country or region level) location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, OS version, app version and language;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed or tapped, and diagnostic and performance data about ad delivery.

This data is collected and shared with Google and, where you have consented, with Google's advertising partners, for advertising and analytics purposes. Razion itself does not read or store any of it; we see only aggregated, non-identifying reports.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain US states, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads and lets you decline. You can change that choice at any time from the Privacy Options button in the game's settings. On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation under Settings → Google → Ads. On iOS the system asks, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether Razion may track you across other apps; if you decline, AdMob does not receive your IDFA, and you can change the answer at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Razion is playable without personalised ads.

Ad attribution on iOS. The iOS version declares Google's SKAdNetwork identifier, which lets Apple report to advertisers, in aggregate and without identifying you, that an ad led to an install.

Ad-free purchase. Buying "Remove Ads" stops Razion from requesting or showing ads, and with it the ad-related collection described above.

Google describes its own handling of this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (the ad-free upgrade and additional airframes) are handled entirely by the store you bought the game from: Google Play Billing on Android, and Apple's App Store (StoreKit) on iOS. The store processes your payment and holds your payment details. Razion receives only a purchase record and the product identifier, which it uses to unlock what you bought and to restore your purchases on reinstall or on another device signed into the same Google account or Apple ID. We keep a record of purchases in the local save file for that purpose. Purchase history is not shared with anyone other than the store that processed it.

6. Data we do not collect

Razion does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera data, or the contents of your files. It does not include analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social SDKs, and it has no chat or user-generated content.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Local save file: kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.
  • Cloud profile (Android): kept in your Google account until you delete it (section 3).
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play or Apple under their own terms; the local copy is deleted with the save file.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google under Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID (Android) or withdraw tracking permission (iOS) at any time.

We hold no data about you that we could delete on request, but if you contact us we will help you find the right Google or Apple control.

9. Children

Razion is rated for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help you have it removed.

10. Security

Communication with Google's and Apple's services uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections. The local save file is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified device.

11. International transfers

Google's and Apple's services may process data on servers outside your country, including the United States. Both maintain safeguards for such transfers as described in their privacy policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes to this page and update the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the game's Play Store and App Store listings.
