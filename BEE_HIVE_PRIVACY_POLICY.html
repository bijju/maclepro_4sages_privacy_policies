Beehive Jam Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 September 2026
Last updated: 29 October 2026
Publisher: MACLEPRO INC ("we", "us")
Developer: 4 Sagez Studios Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: the Beehive Jam (Bee Hive) puzzle game for Android (package com.foursagez.beehive) and for iOS and iPadOS (bundle com.foursagez.beehive)

This policy explains what information Beehive Jam handles, why, and what choices you have. Beehive Jam is designed to keep your data on your device. We operate no player servers and hold no player accounts. Where a Google or Apple service is involved, we say so plainly below. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Summary

  • Beehive Jam does not require you to create an account, and has no player servers of its own.
  • Your level progress, Gold, Gems, helpers, streaks, achievements, settings and purchases are stored in a save file on your device.
  • Optionally, a copy of your game progress can be kept in your own Google Play Games account (Android) or Game Center / iCloud account (iOS), so it can be restored on a new device. That copy is held by Google or Apple in your account. We cannot access it.
  • The game shows advertising through Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier and related data. Buying "Remove Ads" stops interstitial ads permanently; rewarded ads are only ever shown when you choose to watch one.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing on Android and by Apple's App Store on iOS. We never see your payment details.
  • Beehive Jam contains no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social SDKs, no chat, and no multiplayer.

2. Information stored on your device

Beehive Jam keeps a local save file containing your campaign progress, Gold and Gem balances, helper inventory, daily and weekly reward and mission state, achievements, settings (sound, music, haptics, reduced motion, text size), your advertising consent choice, and a record of your purchases. The save file also contains a random install identifier: 16 random bytes generated on your device when the game is first run. It is not derived from your hardware, phone number or any account, and it is not sent anywhere. It exists only so the game can keep its own save file consistent.

The game also writes a local gameplay event log (for example "level started", "ad completed", "purchase succeeded") to a file in its private storage to help diagnose problems. Beehive Jam ships no analytics service, so this log stays on your device and is never uploaded. It is capped in size and older entries are discarded.

All of this is stored in the app's private storage. Apart from the optional cloud save copy described in section 3, it never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes the on-device copy.

3. Cloud save (optional)

Beehive Jam can keep a copy of your game progress in your own game account, so you can continue on a new or reinstalled device. This uses the platform's own saved-games service:

  • Android: Google Play Games Services "Saved Games". The copy is stored in a hidden app-data folder in your Google Drive that only Beehive Jam can use. It does not appear among your Drive files and does not use other apps' data.
  • iOS and iPadOS: Apple Game Center saved games, stored in your iCloud account.

What the copy contains. Your campaign progress, Gold and Gem balances, helpers, streaks, missions, achievements, settings and total play time, plus a timestamp used to tell which copy is newer. It does not contain your purchase records or "Remove Ads" entitlement (these are always re-checked with Google Play or the App Store), your advertising consent choice, or the random install identifier from section 2.

What we receive. Nothing. The copy is stored by Google or Apple in your account and is read back only by the game on your devices. Beehive Jam does not read or store your name, email address, gamer name, profile picture or friends list, and we have no access to your Google Drive or iCloud.

When it is used. On Android, the game connects to Google Play Games when it starts. If your device is already signed in to Play Games, this happens automatically; otherwise you can sign in from Settings → Cloud save in the game. On iOS, the game uses the Game Center account signed in on your device. If you are not signed in to Play Games or Game Center, nothing is uploaded and the game works exactly as before, with your progress on your device only. While you are signed in, the game updates the cloud copy after you make progress, at most about once every 45 seconds, and you can also press Sync now in Settings. If two devices hold very different progress, the game asks you which one to keep.

Google and Apple handle this data under their own terms: see the Google Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Network connections the game makes

Apart from cloud save (section 3), advertising (section 5) and store purchases (section 6), Beehive Jam makes two kinds of request:

  • Time check. The game sends a request to https://www.google.com/generate_204 and reads only the date from the reply, so that daily rewards and timers cannot be altered by changing the device clock. The request contains no information about you.
  • Game configuration. The game may download a small configuration file used to tune gameplay values (for example reward amounts). The request contains no information about you. As with any web request, the server that hosts the file sees your IP address for the duration of the request.

All of these connections use HTTPS. Core gameplay works fully offline.

5. Advertising

Beehive Jam shows interstitial ads between levels (never before Level 8, never during a level, and never if you own "Remove Ads") and optional rewarded video ads, served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in those ads, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID; on iOS the IDFA only where you have allowed tracking) and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate (country or region level) location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, OS version, app version and language;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed or tapped, and diagnostic and performance data about ad delivery.

This data is collected and shared with Google and, where permitted, with Google's advertising partners, for advertising and analytics purposes. Beehive Jam itself does not read or store any of it; we see only aggregated, non-identifying reports.

Your choices. Where local law requires it (including the EEA, UK and Switzerland), Beehive Jam asks for your advertising choices on first launch through Google's consent form. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the game. On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation under Settings → Google → Ads (on newer devices, Settings → Privacy → Ads). On iOS, Beehive Jam does not present Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not track you across other companies' apps; ads on iOS are served without your IDFA. If you decline personalised ads you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised, and Beehive Jam is fully playable.

Rewarded ads. A rewarded ad is only requested when you tap the option to watch one in exchange for Gold, Gems or a helper. The game states the exact reward before the ad plays and limits how many rewarded ads you can watch per day. Missions never require you to watch an ad. You are never required to watch one.

Ad attribution on iOS. The iOS version declares Google's SKAdNetwork identifiers, which let Apple report to advertisers, in aggregate and without identifying you, that an ad led to an install.

Ad-free purchase. Buying "Remove Ads" stops Beehive Jam from requesting or showing interstitial ads, and with it the ad-related collection described above, except for any rewarded ad you choose to watch.

Google describes its own handling of this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (Gem packs, the Starter Pack and the one-time "Remove Ads" upgrade) are handled entirely by the store you got the game from: Google Play Billing on Android, and Apple's App Store (StoreKit) on iOS. The store processes your payment and holds your payment details. Beehive Jam receives only a purchase record and the product identifier, which it uses to grant what you bought and to restore the "Remove Ads" upgrade on reinstall or on another device signed into the same Google account or Apple ID. We keep a record of purchases in the local save file for that purpose, including a ledger of Gems bought and spent so that a refund can be applied correctly. Purchase records are not included in the cloud save copy, and purchase history is not shared with anyone other than the store that processed it.

7. Data we do not collect

Beehive Jam does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera data, or the contents of your files. It does not include analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social SDKs, has no chat, no multiplayer and no user-generated content, and sends no push notifications. The only copy of your save data that leaves your device is the optional cloud save copy in your own Google or Apple account (section 3).

9. Retention and deletion

  • Local save file and event log: kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.
  • Cloud save copy (Android): kept in your Google account until you delete it. To delete it, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, find Beehive Jam and choose Delete hidden app data. Deleting your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games settings also removes it, along with your data for every other game.
  • Cloud save copy (iOS): kept in your iCloud account until you delete it. To delete it, open Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage), choose Beehive Jam and delete its data.
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play or Apple under their own terms; the local copy is deleted with the save file.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google under Google's policies; you can withdraw advertising consent from Settings → Privacy options in the game and reset your advertising ID (Android) at any time.

Uninstalling the game does not delete the cloud save copy; that is what lets you restore your progress after reinstalling. If you want everything gone, delete the cloud copy as described above as well. We hold no data about you that we could delete on request, but if you contact us we will help you find the right Google or Apple control.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for personalised advertising, on our legitimate interest in funding the game for non-personalised advertising and for the on-device operation of the game, and on performance of a contract for delivering items you purchase and for the cloud save you choose to use. If you live in California, we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA. Because we hold no account or profile for you, the practical controls are the ones described in sections 3, 5 and 9; for data held by Google or Apple, exercise your rights with them directly using the links in sections 3 and 5. You may also write to us at the address in section 15.

11. Children

Beehive Jam is rated for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help you have it removed.

12. Security

Communication with Google's and Apple's services uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections. The local save file is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified device, and is integrity-sealed against casual editing. The cloud save copy is protected by the security of your Google or Apple account.

13. International transfers

Google's and Apple's services may process data on servers outside your country, including the United States. Both maintain safeguards for such transfers as described in their privacy policies. We do not transfer your personal information internationally ourselves, because we do not receive it.
