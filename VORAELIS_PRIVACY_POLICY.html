Voraelis Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 September 2026
Publisher: MACLEPRO INC ("we", "us")
Applies to: the Voraelis game for Android (package pro.macle.voraelis), for iOS and iPadOS (bundle pro.macle.voraelis), and the Windows, macOS and Linux desktop editions

This policy explains what information Voraelis handles, why, and what choices you have. Voraelis is built to run entirely on your device: it has no account system and no servers of ours. Where a Google or Apple service is involved, we say so plainly below. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Summary

  • Voraelis does not require an account, has no sign-in and no servers of its own.
  • Your campaign progress, house and faction choices, settings, gold, gems and purchase records are stored on your device.
  • The game keeps a small playtest log on your device (session lengths, battles played, tutorial completion). It is never uploaded. You can view it as a plain-text report and share it with us yourself if you choose to.
  • On Android, you can choose to watch a rewarded video ad served by Google AdMob in exchange for in-game gold. Ads never play unless you tap the ad button yourself. The iOS and desktop editions contain no ads.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing on Android and by Apple's App Store on iOS. We never see your payment details. The desktop editions have no real-money purchases.
  • Versus mode connects two devices directly over your own local network. Nothing passes through us.
  • Voraelis contains no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social SDKs, no chat and no user-generated content.

2. Information stored on your device

Voraelis keeps a local save containing your campaign progress, house and faction selections, settings (sound, music, haptics, speed), in-game gold and gems, and a record of what you have purchased or unlocked. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes it. If your device backs up app data to your Google or Apple account, that backup is governed by Google's or Apple's own privacy policy.

Playtest log. The game also appends a few lines to a local file at battle boundaries: when a session starts and ends, how long it lasted, which battle number was played and whether it was won, and whether the tutorial was completed. The log contains no name, identifier, location or device details. It is written only to the app's private storage and is never transmitted. The Commander screen has a "Copy playtest report" button that turns the log into a short plain-text summary on your clipboard; what you do with that text is entirely your choice. The log is deleted with the rest of the game's data.

3. Advertising (Android only)

The Android edition offers optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. An ad is only requested and shown when you tap the rewarded-ad button; the game never shows forced or interstitial ads. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in those ads, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID) and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate (country or region level) location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, OS version, app version and language;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed or completed, and diagnostic and performance data about ad delivery.

This data is collected and shared with Google and, where you have consented, with Google's advertising partners, for advertising and analytics purposes. Voraelis itself does not read or store any of it; we see only aggregated, non-identifying reports.

Your choices. You can simply never tap the ad button; the game is fully playable without watching ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain US states, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads and lets you decline. On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation under Settings → Google → Ads.

iOS and desktop. The iOS, iPadOS, Windows, macOS and Linux editions of Voraelis contain no advertising SDK, do not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency and do not declare SKAdNetwork identifiers. If a future update adds ads to those editions, this policy will be updated first.

Google describes its own handling of this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (gem and gold packs, the Starter Pack, the Royal Charter and the War Spoils Vault) are handled entirely by the store you installed the game from: Google Play Billing on Android, and Apple's App Store (StoreKit) on iOS. The store processes your payment and holds your payment details. Voraelis receives only a purchase confirmation and the product identifier, which it uses to credit what you bought and to restore purchases on reinstall or on another device signed into the same Google account or Apple ID. A record of purchases is kept in the local save for that purpose and is not shared with anyone other than the store that processed it. The desktop editions have no real-money purchases.

5. Local multiplayer (Versus mode)

Versus mode lets two devices play against each other over the same Wi-Fi or local network. One device hosts and the other joins by entering the host's local IP address and, if the host set one, a room password. The connection is direct between the two devices; no traffic passes through us or any relay server, and the game does not discover or contact devices outside your network. The room password is checked once during the handshake and is never written to disk. The only data exchanged is battle state (unit positions, orders, race choice, series score). Voraelis requests the Android INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions solely for this local connection and for the store and ad services described above.

6. Data we do not collect

Voraelis does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera data, or the contents of your files. It does not include analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social SDKs, has no leaderboards, chat or user-generated content, and does not use Google Play Games, Game Center, iCloud or any other cloud save.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Local save and playtest log: kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play or Apple under their own terms; the local copy is deleted with the save.
  • Advertising data (Android): retained by Google under Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID at any time.
  • Versus mode data: held in memory only for the duration of the match and discarded when it ends.

We hold no data about you that we could delete on request, but if you contact us we will help you find the right Google or Apple control.

9. Children

Voraelis is rated for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help you have it removed.

10. Security

Communication with Google's and Apple's services uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections. The local save and playtest log are stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified device. Versus mode traffic stays on your local network.

11. International transfers

Google's and Apple's services may process data on servers outside your country, including the United States. Both maintain safeguards for such transfers as described in their privacy policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes to this page and update the effective date above. Material changes, such as adding online features or ads on additional platforms, will also be noted in the game's Play Store and App Store listings.
