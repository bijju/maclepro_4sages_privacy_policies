Dead Level Z Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 September 2026
Publisher: MACLEPRO INC ("we", "us")
Developer: 4 Sagez Studios Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: the Dead Level Z game for Android (package com.foursagez.deadlevelz), for iOS and iPadOS (bundle com.foursagez.deadlevelz), and the Windows and Steam Deck builds

This policy explains what information Dead Level Z handles, why, and what choices you have. Dead Level Z is designed to keep your data on your device. We operate no servers and hold no player accounts. Where a Google or Apple service is involved, we say so plainly below. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Summary

  • Dead Level Z does not require an account, has no sign-in, and has no servers of its own.
  • Your progress, settings, gems, unlocked characters and purchases are stored in a save file on your device. There is no cloud save.
  • The game shows advertising through Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier and related data. Buying "Remove Ads" stops interstitial ads; rewarded ads are only ever shown when you choose to watch one.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing on Android and by Apple's App Store on iOS. We never see your payment details.
  • Optional co-op play connects your device directly to a friend's device on the same local network. No traffic passes through us.
  • Dead Level Z contains no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social SDKs, and no chat.

2. Information stored on your device

Dead Level Z keeps a local save file containing your campaign progress, settings, gem balance, unlocked characters and upgrades, daily-reward timestamps and a record of your purchases. The file is stored in the app's private storage and never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes it.

3. Local co-op play

Dead Level Z offers optional co-op for up to four players. One player hosts on their device and friends join by entering the host's local network address. The connection is made directly between the players' devices over your local network; it does not go through any server operated by us or anyone else.

During a session, the following is exchanged between the participating devices only: the display name you type (up to 16 characters), the character you picked, and the gameplay data needed to keep everyone's game in sync (positions, actions and mission state). As with any direct connection, the other players' devices can see your device's local network address for the duration of the session. If the host sets a password, joiners prove they know it using a salted hash; the password itself is never sent. The link is encrypted with DTLS.

Nothing from a co-op session is stored after it ends, other than your own progress in your own save file. Choose a display name you are comfortable showing to the people you play with.

4. Advertising

Dead Level Z shows interstitial ads between levels and optional rewarded video ads, served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in those ads, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID, or on iOS the IDFA where you have allowed tracking) and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate (country or region level) location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, OS version, app version and language;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed or tapped, and diagnostic and performance data about ad delivery.

This data is collected and shared with Google and, where permitted, with Google's advertising partners, for advertising and analytics purposes. Dead Level Z itself does not read or store any of it; we see only aggregated, non-identifying reports.

Your choices. On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation under Settings → Google → Ads (on newer devices, Settings → Privacy → Ads). On iOS the system asks, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether Dead Level Z may track you across other apps; if you decline, AdMob does not receive your IDFA, and you can change the answer at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where local law requires consent for personalised advertising, Google's tools honour the consent choices available on your device and in your region. Dead Level Z is fully playable without personalised ads.

Rewarded ads. A rewarded ad is only requested when you tap the option to watch one in exchange for gems. The game limits how many rewarded ads you can watch per day. You are never required to watch one.

Ad attribution on iOS. The iOS version declares Google's SKAdNetwork identifiers, which let Apple report to advertisers, in aggregate and without identifying you, that an ad led to an install.

Ad-free purchase. Buying "Remove Ads" stops Dead Level Z from requesting or showing interstitial ads, and with it the ad-related collection described above, except for any rewarded ad you choose to watch.

Desktop builds. The Windows and Steam Deck builds do not load the Google Mobile Ads SDK and show no ads.

Google describes its own handling of this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (gem packs and the "Remove Ads" upgrade) are handled entirely by the store you bought the game from: Google Play Billing on Android, and Apple's App Store (StoreKit) on iOS. The store processes your payment and holds your payment details. Dead Level Z receives only a purchase record and the product identifier, which it uses to grant what you bought and to restore the "Remove Ads" upgrade on reinstall or on another device signed into the same Google account or Apple ID. We keep a record of purchases in the local save file for that purpose. Purchase history is not shared with anyone other than the store that processed it.

6. Data we do not collect

Dead Level Z does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera data, or the contents of your files. It does not include analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social SDKs, has no chat and no user-generated content beyond the co-op display name described in section 3, and sends no push notifications.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Local save file: kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.
  • Co-op session data: held in memory on the participating devices only and discarded when the session ends.
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play or Apple under their own terms; the local copy is deleted with the save file.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google under Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID (Android) or withdraw tracking permission (iOS) at any time.

We hold no data about you that we could delete on request, but if you contact us we will help you find the right Google or Apple control.

9. Children

Dead Level Z is a zombie survival shooter and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help you have it removed.

10. Security

Communication with Google's and Apple's services uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and co-op sessions are encrypted with DTLS. The local save file is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified device, and is integrity-sealed against casual editing.

11. International transfers

Google's and Apple's services may process data on servers outside your country, including the United States. Both maintain safeguards for such transfers as described in their privacy policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes to this page and update the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the game's Play Store and App Store listings.
