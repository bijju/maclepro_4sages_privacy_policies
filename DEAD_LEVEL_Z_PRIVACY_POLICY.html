Dead Level Z Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026
Publisher: MACLEPRO INC ("we", "us")
Developer: 4 Sagez Studios Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: the Dead Level Z game for Android (package com.foursagez.deadlevelz), for iOS and iPadOS (bundle com.foursagez.deadlevelz), and the Windows and Steam Deck builds

This policy explains what information Dead Level Z handles, why, and what choices you have. Dead Level Z is designed to keep your data on your device. We operate no servers and hold no player accounts. Where a Google, Apple or Epic Games service is involved, we say so plainly below. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Summary

  • Dead Level Z does not require an account, asks you to sign in to nothing, and has no servers of its own.
  • Your progress, settings, gems, unlocked characters and purchases are stored in a save file on your device. If you choose to turn on cloud save, a copy is kept in your own Google Play Games or Apple iCloud account.
  • The game shows advertising through Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier and related data. Buying "Remove Ads" stops interstitial ads; rewarded ads are only ever shown when you choose to watch one.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing on Android and by Apple's App Store on iOS. We never see your payment details.
  • Optional co-op play connects your device directly to other players' devices: on the same local network, or online through Epic Online Services, which creates an anonymous player identifier for your device. No game traffic passes through us.
  • Dead Level Z contains no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social SDKs, and no chat.

2. Information stored on your device

Dead Level Z keeps a local save file containing your campaign progress, settings, gem balance, unlocked characters and upgrades, daily-reward timestamps and a record of your purchases. The file is stored in the app's private storage. It leaves your device only if you turn on cloud save (section 3). Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes it.

3. Optional cloud save

You can choose to back up your progress so it carries over to a new device. On Android this uses Google Play Games saved games, which you sign in to from the game's settings; on iOS it uses Game Center saved games, which use the Apple ID already on your device. Cloud save is off until you choose it, and the game plays the same without it.

When it is on, a copy of your gameplay progress is stored in your own account: in your Google Drive app-data folder on Android, or in your iCloud on iOS. It is stored by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies. The copy contains no payment information or purchase receipts (purchases are always restored from the store itself), and it is not sent to us; we cannot read it. You can delete it with the controls Google or Apple provide for that storage.

4. Co-op play

Dead Level Z offers optional co-op and versus modes for up to four players. In every session one player's device hosts and the other players' devices connect to it. No session traffic passes through any server operated by us. There are two ways to connect.

Online co-op (Epic Online Services)

On Android and iOS, the Quick Match, Host a Room and Join Room options use Epic Online Services, provided by Epic Games, Inc., to find other players and connect you to them. Nothing is sent to Epic unless you use one of these options.

  • Anonymous player identifier. The first time you play online, the game asks Epic to create an identifier for your device. No Epic account is needed and you are not asked to sign in. The same identifier is reused whenever you play online on that device. It is not linked to your name, email address, or Google or Apple account.
  • Device description. When the identifier is created, the game tells Epic your device's operating system and model name (for example "Android Pixel 8").
  • Session listing. So that players can find each other, the game publishes a short-lived listing on Epic's service with the host's player identifier, the game mode and, for a private room, its 6-character room code. The listing is removed when the session ends.
  • Display name. The name of the operative you are playing (for example "Ryo Maverick"). There is no option to type your own name.
  • Network connection. Epic's service sees your device's IP address when you connect, as any internet service does. Players are connected directly where possible, in which case the other players in your session can also see your IP address. Where a direct connection is not possible, Epic relays the traffic between players. Connections are encrypted.

Epic processes this information under its own privacy policy: Epic Games Privacy Policy. The Windows and Steam Deck builds do not include Epic Online Services and offer local network co-op only.

Local network co-op

You can also play with friends on the same local network: one player hosts and friends join by entering the host's local network address. The connection is made directly between the players' devices over your local network and does not go through any server operated by us or anyone else. As with any direct connection, the other players' devices can see your device's local network address for the duration of the session. If the host sets a password, joiners prove they know it using a salted hash; the password itself is never sent. The link is encrypted with DTLS.

What other players receive

In either kind of session, the following is exchanged between the participating devices: the name of the operative you are playing, the character and weapon you picked, your score, and the gameplay data needed to keep everyone's game in sync (positions, actions and mission state). Nothing from a co-op session is stored after it ends, other than your own progress in your own save file.

5. Advertising

Dead Level Z shows interstitial ads between levels and optional rewarded video ads, served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in those ads, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID, or on iOS the IDFA where you have allowed tracking) and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate (country or region level) location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, OS version, app version and language;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed or tapped, and diagnostic and performance data about ad delivery.

This data is collected and shared with Google and, where permitted, with Google's advertising partners, for advertising and analytics purposes. Dead Level Z itself does not read or store any of it; we see only aggregated, non-identifying reports.

Your choices. On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation under Settings → Google → Ads (on newer devices, Settings → Privacy → Ads). On iOS the system asks, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether Dead Level Z may track you across other apps; if you decline, AdMob does not receive your IDFA, and you can change the answer at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where local law requires consent for personalised advertising, the game shows Google's consent form before its first ad request, and a Privacy Options entry in the game's settings lets you change your choice at any time. Dead Level Z is fully playable without personalised ads.

Rewarded ads. A rewarded ad is only requested when you tap the option to watch one in exchange for gems. The game limits how many rewarded ads you can watch per day. You are never required to watch one.

Ad attribution on iOS. The iOS version declares Google's SKAdNetwork identifiers, which let Apple report to advertisers, in aggregate and without identifying you, that an ad led to an install.

Ad-free purchase. Buying "Remove Ads" stops Dead Level Z from requesting or showing interstitial ads, and with it the ad-related collection described above, except for any rewarded ad you choose to watch.

Desktop builds. The Windows and Steam Deck builds do not load the Google Mobile Ads SDK and show no ads.

Google describes its own handling of this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

6. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (gem packs, the "Remove Ads" upgrade, the Starter Pack and the seasonal Commander's Pass) are handled entirely by the store you bought the game from: Google Play Billing on Android, and Apple's App Store (StoreKit) on iOS. The store processes your payment and holds your payment details. Dead Level Z receives only a purchase record and the product identifier, which it uses to grant what you bought and to restore one-time purchases ("Remove Ads", the Starter Pack and the Commander's Pass) on reinstall or on another device signed into the same Google account or Apple ID. We keep a record of purchases in the local save file for that purpose. Purchase history is not shared with anyone other than the store that processed it.

7. Data we do not collect

Dead Level Z does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera data, or the contents of your files. It does not include analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social SDKs, has no chat and no user-generated content, and sends no push notifications.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Local save file: kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.
  • Cloud save copy: kept in your own Google or Apple account until you delete it with the controls Google or Apple provide.
  • Co-op session data: held in memory on the participating devices only and discarded when the session ends. Online session listings on Epic's service are removed when the session ends.
  • Anonymous online player identifier: retained by Epic Games under Epic's policies. To ask for it to be deleted, contact us and we will pass your request to Epic, or contact Epic directly using the details in its privacy policy.
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play or Apple under their own terms; the local copy is deleted with the save file.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google under Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID (Android) or withdraw tracking permission (iOS) at any time.

We hold no data about you on any server that we could delete on request, but if you contact us we will help you find the right Google, Apple or Epic control.

10. Children

Dead Level Z is a zombie survival shooter and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help you have it removed.

11. Security

Communication with Google's, Apple's and Epic's services uses encrypted connections. Online co-op connections are encrypted by Epic Online Services, and local network co-op sessions are encrypted with DTLS. The local save file is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified device, and is integrity-sealed against casual editing.

12. International transfers

Google's, Apple's and Epic Games' services may process data on servers outside your country, including the United States. Each maintains safeguards for such transfers as described in its privacy policy.
