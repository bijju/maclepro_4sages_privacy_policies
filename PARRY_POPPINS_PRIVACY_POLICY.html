Parry Poppins Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026
Last updated: 29 October 2026
Publisher: MACLEPRO INC ("we", "us")
Developer: 4 Sagez Studios Pvt. Ltd.
Applies to: the Parry Poppins mobile game for Android and iOS (package/bundle id com.foursagez.parrypoppins)

This policy explains what information Parry Poppins handles, why, and what choices you have. Parry Poppins is designed to keep your data on your device. We operate no player servers of our own and hold no player accounts. The game has online multiplayer, advertising, an in-game currency and real-money purchases of that currency, each described below; where a Google or Epic service is involved, we say so. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Summary

  • Parry Poppins does not require you to create an account, and has no player servers of its own.
  • Your settings, player name, match statistics, unlocked cosmetics, gem balance and purchases are stored in a save file on your device. Optionally, a copy can be kept in your own Google Play Games account (Android) so it can be restored on a new device (section 3). That copy is held by Google in your account; we cannot access it.
  • Online multiplayer matches you with another player (a stranger, or a friend you invite with a room code) through Epic Online Services (Epic Games, Inc.). It gives your device an anonymous player id and relays the match between the two devices. Your player name, fighter choice and in-match data are visible to the player you are matched with and pass through Epic's service (section 4).
  • Online matches are always against another person; the game never swaps in a computer opponent.
  • The game shows advertising through Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier and related data. Buying "Remove Ads" stops interstitial ads permanently; rewarded ads are only shown when you choose to watch one (section 6).
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details (section 7).
  • Gems are an in-game currency with no cash value (section 8).
  • Parry Poppins contains no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social SDKs of our own, and no chat.

2. Information stored on your device

Parry Poppins keeps one local save file containing:

  • Settings such as volume levels and control preferences.
  • Player name, which you can set in the Settings menu or in the box on the multiplayer screen. It defaults to "Player" and you can use any name you like; it does not have to be your real one.
  • Match statistics: wins, losses, matches played, rounds won and lost, balloons popped and parries landed.
  • Progress and cosmetics: which fighters and looks you have unlocked and which one is selected, and your campaign progress.
  • Gem balance, whether you own "Remove Ads", and the time you last watched a rewarded ad (used only to space out how often that ad can be offered).
  • The date the profile was created.

All of this is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified device. Apart from the optional cloud save copy described in section 3, it never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes the on-device copy.

3. Cloud save (optional)

On Android, Parry Poppins can keep a copy of your game progress in your own Google account, so you can continue on a new or reinstalled device. This uses Google Play Games Services "Saved Games". Signing in is optional and separate from opening the game; everything works locally without it.

  • Where it is stored. In a hidden app-data folder in your Google Drive that only Parry Poppins can use. It does not appear among your Drive files and does not use other apps' data.
  • What the copy contains. Your settings, player name, statistics, progress, cosmetics and gem balance, plus a timestamp used to tell which copy is newer. It does not contain your purchase records or your "Remove Ads" entitlement; those are restored through Google Play, so a copy from someone else's account can never grant or remove a purchase.
  • What we receive. Nothing. The copy is stored by Google in your account and read back only by the game on your devices. We do not read or store your name, email address, gamer name, profile picture or friends list, and we have no access to your copy.
  • When it is used. After you sign in, the game uploads your save and downloads it on any other device you sign in on. If two devices save without seeing each other's changes, the game keeps whichever has more progress (matches played), asking you to choose only if the two have drifted far enough apart that picking automatically would risk losing something real.

Google handles this data under its own terms: see the Google Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy. Signing in shares your Google Play Games player id with the game and with Google Play Games Services.

4. Online multiplayer (Epic Online Services)

Parry Poppins offers an online mode, using Epic Online Services, a matchmaking and relay service operated by Epic Games, Inc. There are two ways to play online:

  • Find Match matches you with whoever else is searching. The game looks for an open match and, if one exists, joins it; otherwise it opens one and waits for someone else to join.
  • Play with a Friend lets one player host a room and share its six-character room code (shown on screen, with a Copy button); the other player types the code to join. The room code is part of the name of the lobby on Epic's service, so only someone who knows the code can find that room. A friend room is a friendly match: no gems are staked or paid out.

In both cases:

  • Your device signs in to Epic Online Services anonymously. This creates an Epic Online Services player id tied to your device, not to any personal account, email address or password of yours.
  • Once two players are in the same match, your device and the other player's device exchange, through Epic's relay: the player name you chose, your chosen fighter, a ready state, which build of the game you run, a rematch request if you ask for one, and the real-time inputs and timing data needed to keep both sides of the fight in step. The host's device also tells the other device how each round ended.
  • To deliver that connection, Epic's service necessarily sees your device's IP address and the other player's, as any online service does.
  • A search never ends in a match against the computer. If nobody joins, the game keeps waiting until someone does or you cancel, and a cancelled search is refunded (see section 8).

Voice chat (friend rooms only). In a Play with a Friend room, players can talk to each other. If you turn the microphone on (it is off until you do, and the game asks Android for microphone permission first), your voice is sent live to the other player through Epic Online Services' voice relay. Voice is not recorded or stored by us, and is not used for anything other than the live conversation. You can mute your microphone or the other player's sound at any time from the voice panel, and random matches have no voice chat.

We do not operate this matchmaking or relay service ourselves; Epic Games does, under its own privacy policy at epicgames.com/site/en-US/privacypolicy. We only receive the outcome of a match (win or loss) to update your own local statistics; we do not otherwise see or store what happens in an online match.

5. Network connections the game makes

Apart from cloud save (section 3), online multiplayer (section 4), advertising (section 6) and store purchases (section 7), Parry Poppins makes no network requests of its own. The campaign, practice and quick-fight modes work fully offline.

6. Advertising

Parry Poppins shows interstitial ads between matches (never during a fight, and never if you own "Remove Ads") and optional rewarded video ads, served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in those ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID; on iOS the IDFA only where you have allowed tracking) and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate (country or region level) location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, OS version, app version and language;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed or tapped, and diagnostic and performance data about ad delivery.

This data is collected and shared with Google and, where permitted, with Google's advertising partners, for advertising and analytics purposes. Parry Poppins itself does not read or store any of it; we see only aggregated, non-identifying reports.

Your choices. Where local law requires it (including the EEA, UK and Switzerland), Parry Poppins asks for your advertising choices on first launch through Google's consent form, before any ad is requested. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time from Settings → Privacy → Ad choices in the game, which asks you again. If you decline personalised ads, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised, and the game remains fully playable. If the form cannot be shown (for example you are offline), the game starts without waiting for it. You can also reset or limit your device's advertising identifier, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising).

Rewarded ads. A rewarded ad is only requested when you tap the option to watch one in exchange for gems. The game states the reward before the ad plays and spaces rewarded ads out with a cooldown. A match never starts behind an ad: if an interstitial ad is shown before a fight, the fight begins only after you close it.

Ad-free purchase. Buying "Remove Ads" stops Parry Poppins from requesting or showing interstitial ads, and with it the ad-related collection described above, except for any rewarded ad you choose to watch.

Google describes its own handling of this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

7. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (gem packs and the one-time "Remove Ads" upgrade) are handled entirely by the store: Google Play Billing on Android. The store processes your payment and holds your payment details; we never see or store your card number, billing address or name, only the store's confirmation that a purchase completed and which item it was, so the game can credit it. Purchases are not currently offered on iOS. Google's own privacy policy covers the payment step: policies.google.com/privacy.

Gem packs are consumed on purchase and are not refundable through us directly; refund requests go through your Google Play purchase history. "Remove Ads" is restored from Google Play if you reinstall or change device, using Restore Purchases in the game's shop.

8. Virtual currency (gems)

Gems are an in-game currency with no cash value. You earn them by winning an online match, by watching a rewarded video ad (subject to a cooldown so it cannot be watched back to back), or by buying a gem pack with real money (section 7). Finding a random online match costs a fixed number of gems, charged when the search starts and refunded if the search fails, is cancelled, or you leave before an opponent is found; the winner of that match receives double the entry back and the loser's entry is not returned. Matches with a friend through a room code cost and pay nothing. Gems cannot be transferred to another player or account, or redeemed for cash or any other thing of value once credited to your balance.

9. Data we do not collect

Parry Poppins does not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise GPS location, camera data, payment card details, or the contents of your files. The only audio the game handles is live voice in a friend room, if you switch your microphone on (section 4); it is relayed to the other player and never recorded or stored by us. Beyond the advertising identifier described in section 6, the anonymous Epic Online Services player id in section 4 and the Google Play Games player id in section 3, we do not run our own analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDKs. The game has no chat and no user-generated content beyond the player name and fighter choice you pick.

11. Retention and deletion

  • Local save file: kept until you clear the app's data (Android: Settings → Apps → Parry Poppins → Storage → Clear data; iOS: delete and reinstall the app) or uninstall the game.
  • Player name: change or blank it at any time in the game's Settings menu or on the multiplayer screen.
  • Online match data and room codes: discarded when the match or room ends; each device keeps only the result in its own local statistics. Retention of the anonymous player id and any matchmaking records is governed by Epic's own policy.
  • Cloud save copy (Android): kept in your Google account until you delete it. To delete it, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, find Parry Poppins and choose Delete hidden app data. Deleting your Google Play Games profile also removes it. Uninstalling the game does not delete the cloud copy; that is what lets you restore your progress after reinstalling.
  • Advertising identifier and choices: withdraw or change your advertising consent at any time from Settings → Privacy → Ad choices in the game, and reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device's settings (section 6); the data itself is held by Google under its own policies, not by us.
  • Purchase records: retained by Google as part of your Play purchase history, governed by Google's own policy, not ours; the local copy is deleted with the save file.

We hold no data about you that we could delete on request. If you want everything gone, clear the app's data or uninstall the game, and delete the cloud copy as described above.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no account and no server-side profile for you, we do not hold personal information about you that we could look up, export or delete. The practical controls available to you are: change or withdraw your advertising consent from Settings → Privacy → Ad choices in the game; reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings; change or blank your player name in the game; delete everything the game has stored by clearing its data or uninstalling it; delete the optional cloud copy as described in section 11; and exercise your rights over data held by Google (advertising, purchases, cloud save) or Epic (online multiplayer) through them directly, using the links in sections 3, 4 and 6. If you live in California, we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA. To make a request or ask a question, contact us at the address in section 17 and we will respond within the period required by law.

13. Children

Parry Poppins is a cartoon fighting game about popping balloons, with no realistic violence. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game does show advertising (section 6), sells in-app purchases (section 7), and offers online multiplayer with strangers (section 4); it is intended for a general audience old enough to use those features responsibly, not for young children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will act on it.

14. Security

Communication with Google's and Epic's services uses encrypted (HTTPS or equivalent) connections. The local save file is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified device. Online multiplayer uses Epic Online Services' own relay and, where supported, its encrypted transport; advertising uses Google's Mobile Ads SDK; purchases and cloud save go through Google Play's own systems. We do not operate any server of our own for the game to contact.

15. International transfers

We hold no personal data of our own, so we make no international transfer of it. Epic Online Services, Google AdMob, Google Play Billing and Google Play Games Services may process data on servers outside your country, including the United States, as part of operating their own services, under the safeguards described in their privacy policies linked above.
