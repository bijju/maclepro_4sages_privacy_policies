Parry Poppins Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 September 2026
Publisher: MACLEPRO INC ("we", "us")
Applies to: the Parry Poppins mobile game for Android (package com.parrypoppins.game)

This policy explains what information Parry Poppins handles, why, and what choices you have. Parry Poppins is built to keep everything on your device. It has no servers, no accounts, no advertising and no in-app purchases. The only time any information leaves your device is when you choose to play a local-network match against another player, and then it goes only to that player. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Summary

  • Parry Poppins does not require an account and we operate no servers.
  • Your settings, chosen player name, match statistics and unlocked cosmetics are stored in a save file on your device.
  • The game contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no sign-in, no cloud save and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Optional two-player matches run directly between two devices on the same Wi-Fi network, or over an address you enter by hand. Your player name and fighter choice are shown to the other player; nothing passes through us.
  • We never see your name, your device, your network address or how you play.

2. Information stored on your device

Parry Poppins keeps one local save file. It contains:

  • Settings such as volume levels and control preferences.
  • Player name, which you can set in the Settings menu. It defaults to "Player" and you can use any name you like; it does not have to be your real one.
  • Match statistics: wins, losses, matches played, rounds won and lost, balloons popped and parries landed.
  • Cosmetics: which fighters and looks you have unlocked and which one is selected.
  • The date the profile was created.

The file is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified device. It never leaves your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game deletes it.

3. Local-network multiplayer

Parry Poppins offers an optional two-player mode over a direct network connection. One player hosts and the other joins by entering the host's network address. The connection is made straight between the two devices; there is no matchmaking service, no relay and no server of ours in between.

While a match is connected, each device sends the other:

  • the player name from the Settings menu, so it can be shown in the lobby and on the in-game scoreboard;
  • the chosen fighter and ready state;
  • the game inputs and timing data needed to keep both sides of the fight in step;
  • a build version number, so that two incompatible versions refuse to play rather than desynchronise.

As with any direct connection, the other player's device can see your device's network address, and yours can see theirs. On a home Wi-Fi network that is a private address that is not reachable from the internet. If you choose to forward a port on your router and share your public address to play with someone elsewhere, that person can see it; only do so with people you trust.

Nothing exchanged in a match is stored by the other device beyond the session, except the result, which each device records in its own match statistics. We have no access to any of it. The game requests the Android internet permission solely to make these connections.

4. Data we do not collect

Parry Poppins does not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera data, advertising identifier, or the contents of your files. It does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social SDKs, has no chat, and has no user-generated content beyond the player name you choose.

Parry Poppins's data flows, as declared to Google Play:

No data is sent to us or to any third party. Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete on request; the deletion routes are in section 6.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local save file: kept until you clear the app's data (Android Settings → Apps → Parry Poppins → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the game.
  • Player name: change or blank it at any time in the game's Settings menu.
  • Multiplayer session data: discarded when the match ends. The other player's device keeps only the match result in its own statistics.

We hold no data about you that we could delete on request, but if you contact us we will help with any question about the above.

7. Children

Parry Poppins is a cartoon fighting game about popping balloons, with no realistic violence. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the game collects nothing and sends nothing to us, there is no personal information for us to hold; the only thing a child could share is the player name they choose, and only with a person they play against on the same network. If you have a concern, contact us and we will help.

8. Security

The local save file is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified device. Local-network matches use a direct connection between the two devices and carry only the game data listed in section 3. The game never contacts any server of ours or of a third party.

9. International transfers

No data is transferred to us or to any third party, so no international transfer takes place. The only data movement is between the two devices in a local-network match, wherever they happen to be.

10. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes to this page and update the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the game's Play Store listing.
