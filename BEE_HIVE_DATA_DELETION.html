Beehive Jam: How to Delete Your Data

App: Beehive Jam (Bee Hive), package com.foursagez.beehive
Publisher: MACLEPRO INC
Developer: 4 Sagez Studios Pvt. Ltd.

Beehive Jam has no accounts and no player servers. Everything the game stores about you lives in a save file on your own device, and the only third party that receives any data is Google AdMob, for advertising. This page explains how to delete each of those.

1. Delete the data stored on your device

Uninstalling Beehive Jam deletes the complete save file and the local event log. There is no copy anywhere else, so nothing remains after uninstalling.

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps (or Apps & notifications) and select Beehive Jam.
  3. Tap Uninstall.

To delete your data while keeping the game installed, choose Storage on the same screen and tap Clear storage (or Clear data). The game will start fresh the next time you open it.

2. Delete or reset advertising data held by Google

Google AdMob may hold your device's advertising ID and ad interaction data. Beehive Jam cannot delete that for you, but your device controls it directly.

  1. Open Settings → Google → Ads (on newer devices, Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  2. Tap Delete advertising ID to remove the identifier, or Reset advertising ID to get a new one that is not linked to the old data.

You can also withdraw advertising consent inside the game at Settings → Privacy options. For rights over data Google holds, see Google's privacy policy.

3. Purchase records

Your purchase history is held by Google Play under Google's own terms and is tied to your Google account, not to the game. Beehive Jam keeps only a local copy, which is deleted with the save file in step 1.
