Beehive Jam: How to Delete Your Data

App: Beehive Jam (Bee Hive), package com.foursagez.beehive
Publisher: MACLEPRO INC
Developer: 4 Sagez Studios Pvt. Ltd.
Last updated: 26 September 2026

Beehive Jam has no accounts of its own and no player servers. The game stores your progress in a save file on your device. If you use cloud save, a copy of your progress is also kept in your own Google Play Games account (Android) or Game Center / iCloud account (iOS). The only third party that receives data for its own purposes is Google AdMob, for advertising. This page explains how to delete each of those.

1. Delete the data stored on your device

Uninstalling Beehive Jam deletes the save file and the local event log from your device.

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps (or Apps & notifications) and select Beehive Jam.
  3. Tap Uninstall.

To delete your data while keeping the game installed, choose Storage on the same screen and tap Clear storage (or Clear data).

If you use cloud save, uninstalling or clearing storage does not delete the cloud copy, and the game will restore your progress from it the next time you sign in. To remove your progress completely, also follow step 2.

2. Delete your cloud save copy

If you have never signed in to Google Play Games or Game Center with Beehive Jam, there is no cloud copy and you can skip this step.

Android (Google Play Games): the copy is kept in a hidden app-data folder in your Google Drive.

  1. On a computer, open Google Drive and sign in with the Google account you use for Play Games.
  2. Click the Settings (gear) icon, then Settings → Manage apps.
  3. Find Beehive Jam, click Options and choose Delete hidden app data.

Alternatively, deleting your Play Games profile (Google Play Games settings → Delete Play Games account & data) removes the copy together with your data for every other game.

iOS and iPadOS (Game Center / iCloud):

  1. Open Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage).
  2. Choose Beehive Jam and delete its data.

Delete the cloud copy before reinstalling the game. If the game is still installed and signed in when you delete it, it will upload your current progress again the next time you play; clear the game's storage first (step 1), then delete the cloud copy.

3. Delete or reset advertising data held by Google

Google AdMob may hold your device's advertising ID and ad interaction data. Beehive Jam cannot delete that for you, but your device controls it directly.

  1. Open Settings → Google → Ads (on newer devices, Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  2. Tap Delete advertising ID to remove the identifier, or Reset advertising ID to get a new one that is not linked to the old data.

You can also withdraw advertising consent inside the game at Settings → Privacy options. For rights over data Google holds, see Google's privacy policy.

4. Purchase records

Your purchase history is held by Google Play (or Apple) under their own terms and is tied to your store account, not to the game. Beehive Jam keeps only a local copy, which is deleted with the save file in step 1. Purchase records are never included in the cloud save copy.
