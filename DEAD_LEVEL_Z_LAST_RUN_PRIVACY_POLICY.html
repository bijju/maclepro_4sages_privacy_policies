Privacy Policy for Dead Level Z: Last Run

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Dead Level Z: Last Run (the "Game") on Android or iOS. The Game is published by Maclepro Inc and developed by 4 Sagez Studios Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us"). Store identifier: com.foursagez.deadlevelz.lastrun.

The short version: this version of the Game does not ask you to create an account, does not connect to a server of ours, and does not send your personal information to us. Your progress is saved on your device and, if you are signed in to Google Play Games or Game Center, a copy is kept in your own Google or Apple account so it can be restored on another device. The companies that receive data when you play are Google (for ads and, on Android, Play Games cloud save), Apple (on iOS, Game Center cloud save) and Google or Apple (for purchases).

1. Information we collect

We collect nothing ourselves. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, camera or microphone. The Game keeps a small gameplay statistics log on your device to balance difficulty; it contains no identifiers, never leaves your device, and you can switch it off in Settings.

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress, survivors, upgrades, in-game currency, rewards, settings, ad frequency counters and a local record of your purchases are saved in the Game's private storage on your device. Deleting the Game deletes this data from your device, and we hold no copy that could restore it (see section 3 for the cloud copy kept in your own Google or Apple account).

3. Cloud save (Google Play Games and Game Center)

When you are signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS), the Game keeps a copy of your game progress in your own account so you can continue on a new or reinstalled device: on Android through Google Play Games saved games, stored in the hidden app-data folder of your Google Drive; on iOS through Game Center saved games, stored in your iCloud account. The copy contains your game progress only (survivors, upgrades, in-game currency and resources, missions, records and similar game statistics). It does not contain your settings, your purchase records or your ad counters, which stay on the device.

This copy goes directly between the Game and Google or Apple. We do not receive it, and we cannot see or change it. Google and Apple handle it under their own policies (Google, Apple). Signing in also lets Google or Apple know that you play the Game. The Game does not read your Play Games or Game Center name or player ID.

On Android, Google Play Games signs you in automatically when the Game starts if you have a Play Games profile; otherwise you can sign in from Settings > Cloud save. If you are not signed in, nothing is uploaded. You can turn off automatic sign-in or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings. On iOS, Game Center signs in with your Apple ID if Game Center is turned on in your device's settings; you can turn it off there, and delete the copy through your device's iCloud storage settings.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads through Google AdMob. When ads are requested, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, device information, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data, to serve, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud. This data is collected by Google under its own policies; we only receive aggregate reports. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps.

Where required (the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws), the Game asks for your choice through Google's consent tool before ads are requested. You can change it at any time in Settings > Privacy options. On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. Buying Ad-Free stops interstitial ads.

5. In-app purchases

The Game offers optional purchases: gem packs, Ad-Free, a Starter Pack and a seasonal Season Pass. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your card number, billing address or payment details. The store tells the Game which products you own; Restore Purchases asks the store to confirm them again.

6. Time checks

To keep daily rewards and seasons fair, the Game reads the current time from Cloudflare and Google over HTTPS. These requests carry no game data, account data or identifiers.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Your rights and data deletion

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To remove the data on your device, uninstall the Game. For data held by Google or Apple, including the cloud save copy in your Google Play Games or Game Center account, use the controls they provide.

9. Changes

If a future version adds features that handle personal data, such as online accounts, we will update this policy before releasing it and change the date above.
